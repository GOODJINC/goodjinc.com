Quick Tray is a desktop-only Obsidian community plugin that lets you hide Obsidian to the Windows system tray instead of quitting it. From the tray menu or global shortcuts, you can open or hide Obsidian, create a quick note, or open Search and Quick Switcher.

Configure the quick-note folder and filename pattern, global shortcuts, and the tray icon's left-click action. Filename patterns support date, time, timestamp, and title tokens. The plugin is in English by default and uses Korean when Obsidian is set to Korean.

Install and enable `Quick Tray` from Obsidian's Community plugins browser. It requires Obsidian 1.8.7 or later.
